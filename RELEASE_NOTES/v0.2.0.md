## What's new in v0.2.0

This is the first release Humdrum self-updates into. Future versions arrive automatically — flip on **Settings → Dictation → Check for updates automatically**, or run **App menu → Check for Updates…** any time.

### Dictation got a real workflow

- **Push-to-talk mode.** Hold ⌥Space while you speak; release to paste. Pick toggle vs. push-to-talk in **Settings → Dictation → Activation**.
- **Pause and cancel.** ⌥P pauses an in-flight toggle dictation without losing your buffer; Escape cancels and discards the unflushed tail with a brief red flash.
- **Mid-session paste.** In toggle mode, phrases land as you pause between them instead of at the very end. The orb keeps listening for follow-up speech, so a long thought becomes a sequence of paste-as-you-go chunks rather than one giant flush at the end.
- **Smarter orb.** Breathing animation, audio-reactive levels, "Transcribing…" indicator while the tail Whisper pass runs after a long push-to-talk hold.

### Paste that actually lands

Humdrum now pastes via the same clipboard + ⌘V cascade that Wispr Flow and Superwhisper default to. ⌘V is the single OS-blessed insert event that every mainstream app — Chrome, Electron, Office, JetBrains, Safari, Terminal — actually accepts. The previous Accessibility-API path silently dropped on too many of those targets; the new cascade also detects when an app accepts the paste event but drops it on the floor (looking at you, Electron) and surfaces a "couldn't paste — text on your clipboard, ⌘V to insert" pill so you always know where the dictation went.

### Quality of life

- **Diagnostics.** Settings → Diagnostics reveals local-only logs in Console for triaging issues. Logs include timings, character counts, and outcome flags — never the transcribed text itself.
- **Wake-from-sleep re-warm.** The first ⌥Space after closing your lid is no longer slow; Humdrum re-warms the model on wake.
- **Per-session corrections capture.** Edits you make to a finished transcript are recorded for an upcoming learning-loop feature that biases Whisper toward your real vocabulary.

### Fixes worth calling out

- **No more dropped words on long push-to-talk holds.** A silence-padding fix gives Whisper the segment-end cue it needs to commit your last sentence.
- **Fast re-press is reliable now.** A clutch of race conditions around overlapping start/stop in push-to-talk would occasionally leave the orb invisible, the manager stuck recording, or `manager_busy` errors blocking new dictations until app relaunch. All resolved.
- **No more non-breaking-space leaks.** Holding ⌥Space used to insert U+00A0 into the focused field while you held the keys; the new Carbon-routed hotkey swallows the keystroke entirely.
