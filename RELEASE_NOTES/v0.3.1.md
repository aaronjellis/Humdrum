## What's new in v0.3.1

A focused fix for noise-tag hallucinations leaking into dictated text.

### Mutter (push-to-talk dictation) & Meeting recorder

- **No more `[BLANK_AUDIO]` / `[MUSIC]` in your text.** Whisper's non-speech
  annotation tags — `[BLANK_AUDIO]`, `[Music]`, `[inaudible]`, `(laughing)`,
  `♪` and friends — are now stripped wherever they appear in the transcript,
  not just when a slice was *only* a tag. "send it to the team [BLANK_AUDIO]"
  now pastes as "send it to the team". Bracketed spans are removed
  unconditionally (Whisper only brackets annotations, never spoken words);
  parenthesized spans are removed only when the content matches a known
  annotation vocabulary, so real parenthetical words survive.
- **Decorated hallucinations drop too.** A silence hallucination wrapped in
  tags ("♪ Thanks for watching. ♪") is now recognized and dropped entirely.
- **Noise filter Off is untouched.** As before, the Off level shows exactly
  what Whisper emitted, tags and all.

### Under the hood

- The hallucination filter now lives in one place (`HumdrumCore.NoiseSanitizer`,
  fully unit-tested — 31 tests) instead of three drifting copies across the
  live and finalize paths.
